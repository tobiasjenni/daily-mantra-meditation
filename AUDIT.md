## Historical index-based audit (superseded as a fidelity claim)

# Audit of the 112 meditation entries

Reviewed 17 September 2026.

## Findings

- All 112 numbers occur exactly once; the original Baba Nam Kevalam practice remains separate.
- 30 guides required a correction or clarification: 3, 5, 8, 13, 15, 17, 18, 23, 28, 38, 41, 43, 44, 45, 47, 48, 53, 58, 66, 70, 71, 75, 77, 81, 83, 87, 94, 108, 110, 111.
- 38 entries are explicitly labeled **Adapted practice**. These retain an introductory motif while changing or omitting part of the traditional procedure.
- Every entry now has an individual source/adaptation note and a link to its page in the numbered index.
- 37 entries have optional visual help, using 14 static diagram types. Practices where a diagram would falsely imply a physical mechanism or add little value retain written guidance.

Numerical completeness does not mean these short guides reproduce every traditional procedure. They are original introductory instructions, with omissions and substitutes disclosed. Adult-context entries are non-graphic orientations; specialized breath, sensory-closure, mantra-placement, and subtle-body procedures are not fully taught. This is an editorial content and software audit, not certification by an Osho teacher or a Sanskrit critical edition.

## Method and sources

Each of the 112 existing records was compared with its numbered motif in the [Reps/Osho sequence reproduced in the compiled index, pages 110–115](https://www.satrakshita.com/Books/OshoMeditations.pdf#page=110). This is a secondary compilation, used to identify the sequence rather than authenticate every explanatory detail of Osho's book. The [official book listing](https://shop.osho.com/en/the-book-of-secrets) identifies the book and its 112 methods.

The underlying ancient text was consulted in [Sanskrit on Wikibooks](https://sa.wikibooks.org/wiki/%E0%A4%B5%E0%A4%BF%E0%A4%9C%E0%A5%8D%E0%A4%9E%E0%A4%BE%E0%A4%A8%E0%A4%AD%E0%A5%88%E0%A4%B0%E0%A4%B5) and [GRETIL's transliteration](https://gretil.sub.uni-goettingen.de/gretil/1_sanskr/6_sastra/3_phil/saiva/vijnbhau.htm). [Christopher Hareesh Wallis's translation](https://tantra112.app/vijnana-bhairava-tantra) provides a separately numbered comparison. This audit does not establish a one-to-one Sanskrit verse concordance or verify every chapter of Osho's commentary. Titles, practical examples, diagrams, and editorial notes are newly written, not reproduced book passages.

For each entry, the review considered the attentional object, sequence, setting, important alternatives, whether the guidance changed the underlying method, and whether a visual could clarify rather than misrepresent it. Setup and everyday examples are editorial suggestions. No duration or guarantee of an experience is attributed to the text.

## Material corrections

- **23 versus 36:** An object with other objects receding is now distinguished from a visual impression fading.
- **13 and 43:** Missing alternative approaches are now explained separately. Number 9 identifies the additional motifs while clearly stating that only supported rest is instructed.
- **15:** The omitted head-opening closure is now disclosed, and the substitute is labeled as adapted.
- **41, 44, 45, 47:** Restored strings and combined resonance, the specific U component in the Reps/Osho Aum formulation, inward repetition, and expansion from one's name to other sounds.
- **44:** Explicitly notes the difference from Sanskrit verse 90. The two sound formulations are not silently conflated.
- **70, 71, 75, 94:** Restored the relevant ascending light, connections between centers, light across states, and bodily spaciousness motifs, with simplified imagery identified.
- **83, 108, 110, 111:** Restored the self-inquiry, shared guiding consciousness, spacious-world image, and both pairs of conceptual opposites. Technique 108 was a substantive mismatch, not merely a wording issue.
