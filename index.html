    About the 112 · sources & approach

    The Vigyan Bhairav Tantra is an ancient collection of meditation approaches, explored in Osho’s The Book of Secrets. This library covers all 112 entries in the commonly circulated Reps/Osho sequence. These numbers are not Sanskrit verse numbers; editions arrange and interpret the material differently.

    Titles, steps, and spoken openings are original practical introductions to the traditional ideas, not translations of the verses, reproductions of Osho’s commentary, or Osho recordings. Entries marked “Adapted” use gentler alternatives; other simplifications are explained in the practice notes. Subtle-body language is treated as imagery. These short guides do not replace a full commentary or personal instruction.

    For context, consult the public-domain Sanskrit text, the numbered technique index, or Christopher Hareesh Wallis’s translation.

    There is no single best technique for everyone. Start with something comfortable and stop if the practice feels distressing. Baba Nam Kevalam remains your separate, original practice. The timer’s daily progression is this app’s feature, not a prescription from the book. Everyday, bedtime, and adult-context practices do not need to fill a timed sitting.
